# Hypothesis Notes

## H1: Party-Trust Gap
Trust in political parties and politicians is lower than trust in other institutions.

## H2: Participation Gap
Non-voters show lower democratic satisfaction and lower institutional trust than voters.

## H3: Political Efficacy Gap
Respondents with lower political efficacy show lower democratic satisfaction and institutional trust.

## H4: Social Trust Gap
Respondents with lower social trust show lower democratic satisfaction and institutional trust.

## H5: Economic Security Gap
Respondents who feel economically strained show lower institutional trust.

## H6: Political Orientation Gap
Democratic trust patterns differ across left-right self-placement.
